# Associated Surplus

## From structured credit to structured civilization

SocioProfit is the associated-surplus doctrine inside the SocioProphet economic architecture. It does not mean shareholder-profit maximization, surveillance extraction, or platform capture. It means the measurable surplus produced when persons and communities coordinate attention, knowledge, memory, trust, governance, and accountable automation under legitimate rules.

Economic Prophet is the measurement engine. Heller mesh is the internal economic mechanics. SocioProphet is the governed civilizational intelligence substrate. SocioProfit names the value theory: profit from association, not extraction.

This document extends the Economic Profit framework from enterprise and relationship profitability into community and civilization-scale value measurement. It is a doctrine and measurement boundary, not a payment system, token issuer, exchange, redemption scheme, or settlement layer.

## Core claim

Modern capitalism often treats price as the master measure of value. Markets can be useful calculators, but price is not value. Price is a collapsed market state produced under liquidity, leverage, legal structure, information asymmetry, mandate pressure, technical flows, and power.

Structured credit exposes this directly. A distressed ABS position cannot be understood from screen price alone. A serious valuation requires cash-flow mechanics, collateral durability, servicer behavior, liquidity, tranche priority, legal uncertainty, recovery timing, documentation quality, optionality, stress paths, and scenario-conditioned discounting.

SocioProfit generalizes that lesson.

A person is not a user account. A community is not a market segment. Attention is not ad inventory. Knowledge is not merely intellectual property. Memory is not platform property. Consent is not a checkbox. The deepest undervalued collateral in civilization is trained, sovereign, ethically governed human attention organized into durable communities and augmented by accountable tools.

Associated surplus is the difference between isolated human potential and coordinated human realization.

## Economic Prophet framing

Economic Prophet already measures economic profit through auditable components: revenue, expected loss, expense, funding cost, funding credits, taxes, capital charge, recovery, transfer pricing, relationship effects, object lineage, and audit output.

Associated surplus does not discard that discipline. It extends it.

Instead of valuing only instruments, accounts, products, relationships, and enterprises, the associated-surplus layer measures spheres, communities, knowledge flows, contribution loops, governance quality, and automation leverage. These are not narrative scores. They must be represented as auditable objects with inputs, assumptions, hashes, proofs, and replayable outputs.

## ABS analogy

In structured credit, naive investors see yield. Real valuation inspects:

- collateral pool composition;
- waterfall priority;
- expected loss and unexpected loss;
- servicer incentives;
- documentation defects;
- liquidity and forced-sale risk;
- recovery mechanics;
- legal enforceability;
- optionality and prepayment behavior;
- stress scenarios and regime dependence;
- tranche interaction and correlation.

In SocioProphet, naive platforms see users. Associated-surplus valuation inspects:

- personhood and consent boundaries;
- capability formation;
- trained attention quality;
- knowledge completeness and proof strength;
- memory provenance;
- governance legitimacy;
- community resilience;
- automation leverage;
- trust and evidence reliability;
- exit rights and portability;
- anti-capture controls;
- semantic coherence and concept binding.

The analogy is not decorative. It is the method transfer: value is modeled state under uncertainty, not a single quoted number.

The output should expose both gross creation and deductions:

## Variables

### AttentionQuality

A bounded measure of whether human attention is trained, intentional, non-coerced, and applied to matters with legitimate purpose.

Potential inputs:

- participation cadence;
- task relevance;
- interruption cost;
- review quality;
- learning velocity;
- consented attention scope;
- adversarial manipulation indicators.

### KnowledgeQuality

Knowledge quality should reuse the Heller mesh knowledge functional:

```text
K = C * Q * S * P
```

Where:

```text
C = completeness
Q = quality
S = stability / source strength
P = provenance / proof strength
```

The point is to reduce required collateral or increase confidence only when knowledge is complete, high-quality, stable, and proven. Weak knowledge should not receive a narrative premium.

### GovernanceLegitimacy

A bounded measure of whether the decision process was authorized, contestable, consent-aware, and compliant with community policy.

Potential inputs:

- valid policy references;
- quorum status;
- conflict disclosures;
- contestability bundle presence;
- appeal path;
- consent grants;
- trust-surface declarations.

### EvidenceReliability

A bounded measure of whether evidence is attributable, fresh, reproducible, tamper-evident, and sufficient for the claim being made.

Potential inputs:

- input hashes;
- output hashes;
- signature validity;
- replay completeness;
- source provenance;
- contradiction checks;
- audit-pack validation.

### CoordinationBandwidth

A bounded measure of how effectively a community can move from observation to shared state to lawful action.

Potential inputs:

- decision latency;
- blocked-flow rate;
- gross-to-net compression;
- handoff failure rate;
- semantic binding accuracy;
- policy admission throughput;
- dispute resolution time.

### AutomationLeverage

A bounded measure of useful machine amplification under governance.

Potential inputs:

- model routing efficiency;
- successful governed task completion;
- human review burden reduction;
- policy-denied unsafe action rate;
- replayable automation traces;
- side-effect containment.

### NetworkSurplus

The nonlinear surplus from association: value created by the network that is not reducible to the sum of isolated participants.

Potential inputs:

- cross-sphere productivity effects;
- reusable knowledge artifacts;
- common infrastructure savings;
- mutual aid and support flows;
- learning spillovers;
- federation gains.

### ExtractionLeakage

Value lost to intermediaries, dark patterns, lock-in, surveillance, forced dependency, rent extraction, or non-consensual data capture.

Potential inputs:

- switching cost;
- non-portable identity risk;
- data brokerage exposure;
- opaque ranking/promotion fees;
- platform take-rate without service justification;
- advertising manipulation load.

### CaptureRisk

Risk that the system, community, market, or governance process is captured by capital, state pressure, insiders, vendors, mobs, cult dynamics, or models.

Potential inputs:

- concentration of authority;
- unilateral policy-change risk;
- maintainer bottlenecks;
- opaque funding dependencies;
- regulatory chokepoints;
- model/vendor concentration;
- absence of exit packs.

### EpistemicUncertaintyPenalty

Penalty for unresolved uncertainty, stale evidence, speculative claims, adversarial conditions, contradiction, or unreviewed model output.

Potential inputs:

- epistemic status;
- confidence interval;
- freshness;
- contradiction flags;
- review status;
- adversarial annotations;
- scenario volatility.

### CoordinationFriction

Cost of deliberation, bureaucracy, conflict, integration failure, semantic mismatch, duplicated effort, or governance drag.

Potential inputs:

- unresolved disputes;
- duplicate artifacts;
- schema mismatch;
- policy deadlocks;
- failed handoffs;
- rework;
- onboarding cost.

Associated surplus is measurable through the existing Heller mesh boundary:

| KnowledgeQuality | `K = C * Q * S * P` |
| GovernanceLegitimacy | triparty release state, policy admission, proof lineage |
| EvidenceReliability | audit/proof lineage, replay completeness, contradiction containment |
| CoordinationBandwidth | gross-to-net compression, release correctness, edge throughput |
| AutomationLeverage | champion/challenger, attestation, developer_platform sphere effects |
| NetworkSurplus | inter-sphere EP influence matrix and momentum update |
| ExtractionLeakage | transfer-pricing wedge, platform rent, non-portable value loss |
| CaptureRisk | reserve adequacy, authority concentration, governance sphere risk charge |
| EpistemicUncertaintyPenalty | proof strength, freshness, review state, scenario volatility |
| CoordinationFriction | residual obligations, blocked/review-required flows, bottleneck drag |

The Heller mesh equation remains the operational bridge:

```text
m(t+1) = sigma(W * delta_EP(t) + B * K(t) + A * m(t) - lambda * m(t))
```

Associated surplus explains why the equation matters: the mesh should increase lawful, useful, human-centered coordination momentum while reducing extraction, capture, and epistemic corruption.

## Triparty release discipline

Associated surplus is not recognized merely because activity occurred. A flow becomes constitutionally meaningful only when it survives claim, authority, policy, proof, freshness, replay, and contradiction checks.

The existing Heller triparty quantities apply:

```text
lambda_evid    = economically visible/nettable quantity
lambda_admit   = quantity surviving claim, authority, and policy predicates
lambda_release = quantity also surviving proof, freshness, replay, and contradiction predicates
residual       = gross flow - released common-mode flow
```

Only released common-mode flow should count as high-quality associated surplus. Residuals remain explicit.

The v0.1 boundary is doctrine, measurement, simulation, audit, and internal economic modeling.

## Acceptance criteria for v0.1

A v0.1 associated-surplus implementation is acceptable when it can:

1. represent an associated-surplus run as a deterministic auditable object;
2. compute or ingest all major terms in the associated-surplus scaffold;
3. reuse Heller mesh knowledge quality where applicable;
4. preserve epistemic status and uncertainty penalties;
5. emit input and output hashes;
6. record assumptions and limitations;
7. distinguish gross activity from admitted and released value;
8. expose extraction leakage and capture risk as deductions;
9. validate a synthetic fixture against schema;
10. remain strictly separate from live money movement and token issuance.

## Strategic rule

Economic Prophet measures value. Heller mesh measures internal economic mechanics. SocioProphet governs intelligence, memory, policy, and replay. SocioProfit names the associated surplus produced when humans coordinate better than extractive systems can price them.

The platform must increase the agency of persons and communities faster than it increases the power of administrators, agents, vendors, or states over them.
